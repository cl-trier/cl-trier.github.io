Kai Kugler is a computational and quantitative linguist whose research focuses on how neural language models encode and process linguistic units and structures. He is particularly interested in the relationship between linguistic representations in language models and the differences between human and synthetic texts. Working at the interfaces of lexicon, syntax, semantics, and pragmatics, he uses quantitative and computational methods to investigate how LLMs model linguistic phenomena and where their outputs diverge from human language use.
