Simon Münker is a computational linguist and Research Fellow at the Department of Computational Linguistics at Trier University, where he studies whether language models can stand in for human participants in research. His Ph.D. thesis, "A Critical Examination of Large Language Models as Human Simulacra", tested this across psychometrics and social media simulation. He builds metrics that expose where language models diverge from human behavior, and the frameworks researchers need to use them responsibly. He teaches lectures and seminars in natural language processing and linguistics.
