Simon Werner is a computational linguist and Research Fellow at the Department of Computational Linguistics at Trier University, where his current research focuses on multimodal document representation. His Ph.D. thesis, "Deep Learning for Dynamic Representations of Real-World Entities" (2024), argued that static knowledge graph embeddings fall short when entity behavior changes over time, and enriched entity representations with situational and sequential context. His broader interests include knowledge graph embeddings, temporal knowledge graphs, and explainable AI. He is also the department's study advisor for the B.Sc. in Language, Technology and Media (STeM), including its focus on Artificial Intelligence and Computational Linguistics, and for the M.Sc. in Natural Language Processing, as well as the contact for study abroad.
