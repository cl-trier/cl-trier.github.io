Raghvi Baloni is a Research Fellow at the Department of Computational Linguistics at Trier University. While pursuing her Masters in Computer Science, she realized that research was what she wanted to pursue, which motivated her to start a PhD. Her work so far has examined LLM responses and their reliability as annotators, with a focus on argumentation, explanation quality, and cultural diversity. Having seen how strongly the conclusions about LLM behavior depend on how it is measured, she is currently focusing on mechanistic interpretability, studying what happens to knowledge inside a language model.
