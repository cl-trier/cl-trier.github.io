Christoph Hau is a Research Fellow at the Department of Computational Linguistics at Trier University, where he studies narrative construction across languages and cultures. His Ph.D. research in the BMBF-funded kuKI project examines Russian- and Turkish-language content and asks how language models can be adapted to recognize culturally specific narratives instead of defaulting to an English-centric view. He builds LLM-based annotation pipelines and classifiers, and probes the internal activations of language models to test whether they form stable, human-aligned concept representations. He also teaches in the Master's program in Natural Language Processing and supervises student research.
